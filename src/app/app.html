How we build an AI bot

  1. 01Define the job

    Agree who the bot serves, what it should do and what it must never do.

  2. 02Prepare knowledge

    Collect and clean the documents and data the bot is allowed to use.

  3. 03Design conversations

    Shape the tone, flows and handover points for real situations.

  4. 04Build and connect

    Develop the bot and link it to your website, tools and data.

  5. 05Test with real questions

    Check answers against realistic questions before anyone relies on it.

  6. 06Launch and improve

    Release, review conversations and refine within the agreed scope and business hours.

Handled with care

An AI bot talks to your customers on your behalf, so we treat it like any other part of your product.

  • Data use and privacy are discussed up front, before any data is shared.
  • Bots are tested against realistic questions and edge cases before launch.
  • People stay in the loop, with clear handover to your team.
  • No guaranteed accuracy, savings or results are promised in advance.

AI bot questions

What kind of bot do I need?

It depends on the job. A support bot answers questions, a lead-capture bot gathers enquiries, and an internal assistant helps your staff find information. We help you pick one focused use case to start with.

Will the bot replace my team?

The aim is to handle repetitive questions so your team can focus on work that needs a person. Anything unclear or sensitive is designed to be handed over to your staff.

How do you keep answers accurate?

We ground the bot in information you approve, test it with realistic questions and design it to admit when it does not know. No tool is perfect, so we also plan for review and improvement after launch.

What do I need to share to get started?

Tell us who the bot will serve, the questions people ask most, and where your current information lives. The enquiry form is a good place to start.

How much does an AI bot cost?

Cost depends on the use case, the channels, the integrations and the amount of content involved. We discuss scope and a suitable approach with you first; no fixed estimate is assumed here.

The problem

Slow, confusing or disconnected digital experiences make it harder for customers and teams to get things done. Spreadsheets and repetitive manual steps hide what is happening in a business and make it hard to improve.

Many software projects start with technology and work backwards. The result is a product that looks finished but does not fit the way people actually work.

Our approach

Suraksha Software brings software development and practical technical training together under one brand. We start by understanding people, workflows and constraints, then build solutions that can be maintained and improved.
